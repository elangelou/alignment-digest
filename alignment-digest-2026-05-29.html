🛡️ #1 · Safety Alignment
Towards Context-Invariant Safety Alignment for Large Language Models
arXiv · 2605.20994 · May 2026

Argues that preference-based post-training yields brittle safety that keys off surface form rather than intent, so a benign rephrasing flips a refusal. Proposes training for context-invariant behavior anchored to underlying intent. ⚠️ Caveat: "intent" is itself an under-defined target; the approach may trade one proxy for another.

🧮 #2 · Safety Alignment (Theory)
BSO: Safety Alignment Is Density Ratio Matching
arXiv · 2605.12339 · May 2026

Shows the optimal safe policy's likelihood ratio admits a closed-form decomposition, reducing safety alignment to density-ratio matching. The resulting Bregman Safety Optimization is a single-stage loss family that provably recovers the optimal safe policy. ❓ Open question: how tight do the guarantees stay once the reward/preference model is itself misspecified?

🔭 #3 · Scalable Oversight
Automated Alignment is Harder Than You Think
arXiv · 2605.06390 · May 2026

Makes the case that alignment risk is low in weaker-than-human models, which means today's empirical wins may not transfer past human level — the regime where it actually matters. Calls for new methods to evaluate alignment solutions on superhuman systems. ⚠️ Caveat: largely a position piece; concrete superhuman-eval protocols remain sketched rather than demonstrated.

🔓 #4 · Red Teaming
Metis: Learning to Jailbreak LLMs via Self-Evolving Metacognition
arXiv · 2605.10067 · May 2026

Reframes jailbreaking as inference-time policy optimization in an adversarial POMDP, using a self-evolving metacognitive loop to diagnose a target's defense logic and treat feedback as a semantic gradient. Matters because it automates the discovery of defense-specific attacks rather than reusing static prompts. ❓ Open question: do these attacks survive activation-level monitors, or only string-level filters?

🔓 #5 · Red Teaming
ContextualJailbreak: Evolutionary Red-Teaming via Simulated Conversational Priming
arXiv · 2605.02647 · May 2026

A black-box strategy that runs evolutionary search over simulated multi-turn primed dialogue using five mutation operators (roleplay, scenario, expand, troubleshooting, mechanistic). Reinforces that multi-turn context, not single prompts, is the live attack surface. ⚠️ Caveat: simulated priming may overstate transfer to real conversational deployments.

🔍 #6 · Interpretability
From Token Lists to Graph Motifs: Weisfeiler-Lehman Analysis of SAE Features
arXiv · 2605.06494 · May 2026

Moves SAE feature interpretation beyond flat top-token lists toward graph-motif structure via a Weisfeiler-Lehman analysis, aiming at richer accounts of what "monosemantic" features actually encode. Useful for anyone treating SAE features as oversight primitives. ❓ Open question: do motif-level descriptions yield interventions that change behavior, or just better post-hoc labels?

🤖 #7 · Agentic Safety
Enhancing Agent Safety Judgment for Deceptive Out-of-Distribution Scenarios (ROME)
arXiv · 2605.03242 · May 2026

ROME rewrites known unsafe trajectories into more deceptive evaluation instances while preserving risk labels, yielding 300 challenge cases spanning contextual ambiguity, implicit risk, and shortcut decisions. Directly targets agents' tendency to misjudge safety out of distribution. ⚠️ Caveat: rewrite-based benchmarks risk encoding the rewriter's blind spots as ground truth.
